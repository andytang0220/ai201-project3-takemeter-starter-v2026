wish: proposes a specific change but leaves how it works up to the reader; the core behavior is missing, offered as options or guesses, or the whole change is one clause
spec: describes how the proposed thing works concretely enough that two readers would picture the same feature, but gives no reasoning beyond a motive
pitch: a spec that also engages with at least one consequence: a downside or balance concern, why an existing feature doesn't already solve the problem, or how it interacts with existing mechanics
